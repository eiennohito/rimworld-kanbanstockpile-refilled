Keep supplies where pawns need them without filling every shelf or hauling another handful after each use.

KanbanStockpile-Refilled lets you set how many stacks of each item a stockpile or shelf holds, choose a smaller stack size, and wait until supplies run low before refilling.

[h2]A small supply, replenished when needed[/h2]
Set a steel stack to 60 and Refill From to 50%: pawns start refilling at 30 steel or fewer, then continue above that threshold while skipping small final top-ups.
Deliveries already on their way count toward the limit.

Open the Storage tab to choose Max similar, Refill Until and Refill From.
Set Max similar to Off wherever you want normal hauling.

[h2]Keep your hauling workflow[/h2]
Works with stockpiles, vanilla shelves and Adaptive Storage.
With Pick Up And Haul, Pick Up and Haul - Continued by Zzyxzz, or Pick Up And Haul Reworked, pawns can bring several stacks to limited storage in one trip, including food, hay and cloth.
Items brought back in a pawn's inventory also respect storage limits.
Construction deliveries are unchanged.

[h2]Requirements and existing saves[/h2]
Requires RimWorld 1.7 and Harmony.
Enable Refilled in place of KanbanStockpile or KanbanStockpile Continued; saved storage settings carry over.
After loading a save, a partly filled stack may refill once before resuming the chosen threshold.
Multiplayer is not supported.
